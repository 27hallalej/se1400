April 24, 2026

↓

The National Weather Service in Norman, Oklahoma has issued a preliminary rating of EF4 to the violent tornado which struck Enid, Oklahoma during the afternoon hours of April 23, 2026.

The environment was not necessarily anticipated to support such a violent tornado; mesoscale discussions less than an hour prior to the incident only forecast maximum intensities in the mid-EF3 range. According to KOCO News, no casualties were reported. Still, Vance Air Force Base and surrounding neighborhoods took heavy losses. Facilities at Vance AFB have begun to reopen, and the power has been fully restored.

The tornado reddened over Enid as it swept away homes, trees, and even the dirt from the ground.
Perhaps at the zenith of the tornado's strength, a well-built house was entirely swept away from its foundation.
Ground scouring was observed in the aftermath of the tornado, as seen from a bird's eye view.

This onslaught of severe weather is expected to continue for the next four days, centered mostly around Oklahoma and Kansas.
